# Changelog

## 1.2.8 (2026-10-30)

First release.

- Trust check with three verdicts: Looks fine, Be careful and Red flags, each with three reasons in plain words.
- Receipts: every finding is saved as a page snapshot, a Wayback Machine copy, a UTC timestamp and a SHA-256 hash.
- Keep digging: follows leads up to three steps deep and shows why each step was taken.
- Timeline of every date found in a case.
- Check yourself, with opt-out links for people-search sites and a to-do list.
- Watch for yourself, your own domains or your company. Watches expire after 90 days.
- PDF report with an exhibit table.
- Local AI through Ollama, or rule-based verdicts with no AI at all.
- 3,412 site checks.
- The Windows build: one portable `.exe`.
